Push Pull Legs is simple to plan, easy to recover from, and trains every muscle twice a week. Here’s how to run it for eight weeks.

How heavy to start

Pick a weight you could lift two or three more times than the plan asks. That buffer lets you add weight every week.

“Hit the top of the range on every set, then add 2.5 kg.”
Key takeaways
  • Twice a week per muscle, six days.
  • Start 2–3 reps short of failure.
  • Add 2.5 kg at the top of the range.
