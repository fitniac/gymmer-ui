15g · SPEC BOARD

Workout progress strip

One segment per exercise · 4px · radius 2 · gap 4 · padding 8/16/10. Each block below is one state.

1 · STANDARD — EXERCISE 2 OF 4

Done = accent. Current = hard stop at its share of sets. Pending = track.

2 · SKIPS — EXERCISE 3 OF 4

Skipped exercise = full skip fill (neutral, never accent). Inside the current segment the fill is (done + skipped) / total: done in accent first, skipped after it, rest is track.

3 · CAP — UP TO 8 SEGMENTS

8 is the most: ~41px each at 390, ~34px at 320, still a bar.

4 · 9+ EXERCISES — ONE BAR (EX. 5 OF 12)

Above 8, no segments: one bar, fill = resolved sets / all sets (same accent-then-skip order). The eyebrow carries "exercise N of M". Never scroll the strip.

5 · EXERCISE ADDED MID-WORKOUT — 4 → 5

New segment sits at the exercise's position in the list (end unless inserted). Widths ease 200ms; reduced motion snaps. Swap replaces in place. Temporary ("today only") exercise: dashed outline, matching its dashed set ring.

6 · CARDIO RUNNING — 2:30 OF 5:00

With a target (time or distance), the segment fills live against it, capped at 100%. No target: empty until done, then full.

7 · FINISHED EARLY — STAYS HONEST

Finish never fills the strip. Outstanding sets stay track; the summary says what was done.

TOKENS · BOTH THEMES
DONE · --acc
SKIP · muted 55%
PENDING · --gm-track

Pending is not --raised: in light, --raised (#fbfafa) is lighter than the page and the track disappears. Use the layer’s existing --gm-track (≈ ink 14%, opaque) in both themes; it never reads as a second accent. Skip = new --gm-skip.
