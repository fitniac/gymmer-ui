Bent Over Barbell Row
How to Mistakes Tips Details

COMMON MISTAKES

  • Rounding the lower back under heavy load.
  • Using momentum to swing the weight up.
  • Pulling the bar too high toward the neck.
  • Extending the spine at the top of the rep.

SAFETY

Avoid rounding the spine to prevent serious lower back injury. Do not hyperextend your back at the top of the movement.
