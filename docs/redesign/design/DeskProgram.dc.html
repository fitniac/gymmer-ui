Who it’s for

  • ✓You’ve trained for 3+ months and know the main lifts.
  • ✓You can be in the gym 6 days a week for about an hour.
  • ✓Your main goal is muscle size, with strength as a bonus.

How it progresses

Weeks 1–2 set your working weights at 2–3 reps short of failure. Weeks 3–6 add a set to the first lift of each day and push loads by 2.5 kg when you hit the top of the rep range. Weeks 7–8 cut volume and go heavier. Nova adjusts each week from what you logged.
