How to run Push Pull Legs: your first 8 weeks

What each day looks like, how heavy to start, and the one rule that decides when you add weight.

GGYMMER coaching team · 28 September 2026 · 9 min read
ON THIS PAGE Why the split works Push day How heavy to start Week by week Key takeaways

Push Pull Legs is the most popular split in the gym for a reason: it is simple to plan, easy to recover from, and it trains every muscle twice a week. Here is exactly how to run it for your first eight weeks.

Why the split works

Muscles that work together are trained together. Chest, shoulders and triceps all push; back and biceps pull; legs get their own day. Each group gets two full sessions a week with 72 hours to recover between them.

Push day: the four lifts

Start heavy and finish light. The first lift is where you push the weight; the last two are about the pump and clean reps.

How heavy to start

Pick a weight you could lift for two or three more reps than the plan asks. If the plan says 10, choose a load you could do 12–13 times. That buffer is what lets you add weight every week without stalling.

“Hit the top of the rep range on every set, then add 2.5 kg. That’s the whole rule.”

Week by week

  1. Weeks 1–2Learn the days, find your weights, 3×10 everywhere.
  2. Weeks 3–6Add a fourth set to each day’s first lift. Add weight when you hit 12.
  3. Weeks 7–8Fewer sets, heavier loads. Then test a new best on each first lift.

Key takeaways

  • Every muscle twice a week, six days, about an hour each.
  • Start 2–3 reps short of failure.
  • Add 2.5 kg when every set hits the top of the range.
