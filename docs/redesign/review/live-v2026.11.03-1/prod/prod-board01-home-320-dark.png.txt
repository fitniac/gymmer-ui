ABOUT

What GYMMER does

GYMMER is a strength-training app for people who lift. You create a free account, build a training programme from a library of 3,000+ exercises, log every set, rep and weight as you train, and see your progress charted over time. An AI coach writes and adjusts the programme from what you actually logged. It runs in the browser and as native apps for iOS and Android, and your training history syncs across every device you sign in on.

What we do with your email

The waitlist stores one thing: the email address you type into it. It is used to tell you when GYMMER opens and for nothing else — no newsletter, no passing it to anyone, no advertising. Ask us and it is deleted.
